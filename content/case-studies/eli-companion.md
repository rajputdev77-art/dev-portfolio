## What Eli Is

Eli is a warm, confidential **emotional-support companion** — explicitly *not* therapy, and honest about that line. She speaks the way people here actually talk: Hinglish, switching naturally between English and Hindi. You can type to her or simply talk, and she talks back — a real conversation with natural turn-taking, not a walkie-talkie bot.

Think of her as a *badi behen* — a trusted older sister. Warm, patient, genuinely on your side, but not a yes-machine: she'll gently push back, notice patterns you keep circling, and ask the one question you've been avoiding. She remembers you between sessions, so you never start from zero.

## Who She's For

The people who carry things alone. The ones who won't book a therapist — because of cost, stigma, or "it's not that serious" — but who need somewhere to put the day down. Work stress, family pressure, the 1 AM spiral. Eli sits with you in it, in your language, whenever you need her.

## What a Session Feels Like

- **A calm room, not a chat app.** The session space is designed to feel like sitting down with someone, not messaging a bot.
- **She remembers.** Come back a week later and Eli picks up the thread — the situation you mentioned, the pattern that keeps returning — like someone who was actually listening.
- **She goes deeper, gently.** "I'm fine, anyway—" doesn't just get a nod. Eli notices what's underneath and offers you the door, without ever forcing you through it.
- **Voice that feels human.** No tap-to-talk, no robot cadence. Speak, pause, even go quiet — she holds the space. English and हिंदी, each with its own natural voice.

## The Promises

These are product commitments, not fine print:

- **Not therapy, ever pretended otherwise.** Eli is a companion. She never diagnoses, never gives medical advice, and is upfront about what she is and isn't.
- **If things get heavy, real help is always surfaced.** Verified India crisis helplines (Tele-MANAS, KIRAN, emergency 112) appear automatically whenever a conversation calls for them — reliably, every time.
- **Your sessions are yours.** Conversations are encrypted and no human reads them. Your data never mixes with anyone else's, and you can delete it.
- **Safety doesn't depend on the AI having a good day.** The protective layer is built to work even when the model fails — by design, not by hope.

Built, live in production, and in final pre-launch checks — including legal and clinical review, because a product like this should clear that bar before it asks for anyone's trust. **Public launch: this month.**

## Why I Built Her

India has a vast gap between the people who need someone to talk to and the people who have someone. AI can't and shouldn't replace professional care. It can be the warm, always-available first place to put the weight down. That's Eli.
